## Save your work

- In Power BI: **File** → **Save as** → keep your `.pbix` file.
- In Colab: **File** → **Save a copy in Drive**, and download your `predictions.csv`.

<Warning>
Lab computers may wipe local files when you log out. Save your `.pbix` and `.csv` files to a USB drive or cloud storage before you leave.
</Warning>
