## What you will build

Record labels spend millions promoting songs. If they could estimate which tracks are likely to become hits _before_ release, they could spend that money better. Over two days you will build a small version of exactly that system:

1. A **Power BI dashboard** that explores what makes a song popular.
2. A **machine learning model** in Python that predicts a track's chance of going viral from its audio features.
3. A **loop between the two**, where model predictions come back into the dashboard.

## The dataset

We use the [Spotify Tracks Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/maharshipandya/-spotify-tracks-dataset): about 114,000 tracks across 114 genres, each described by audio features such as danceability, energy and tempo, plus a `popularity` score from 0 to 100.
