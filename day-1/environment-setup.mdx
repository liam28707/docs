<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Power BI Desktop">
    On your computer, find **Power BI Desktop** / **Power BI Web App**in the Start menu. Open it and confirm you reach the blank report canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Google Colab">
    Go to [colab.research.google.com](https://colab.research.google.com), sign in, click **New notebook**. In the first cell, type `print("hello")` and press **Shift + Enter**. You should see `hello`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the dataset">
    Open the [Spotify Tracks Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/maharshipandya/-spotify-tracks-dataset) on Kaggle, sign in, click **Download**, and unzip. You will have a file named `dataset.csv`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the file once">
    Open `dataset.csv` in Excel or Notepad. Notice the columns (`danceability`, `energy`, `popularity`, `track_genre` and so on).
  </Step>
</Steps>
